Engineered for Uncompromising Privacy

How ZEDOX Browser keeps your identity and activity completely invisible on Desktop & Mobile.

Zero History on Disk

Unlike standard browsers that log every search and website visit into local databases, ZEDOX operates entirely in RAM. Nothing touches your drive.

Ephemeral Memory Cookies

Cookies exist only in RAM while the tab remains open. Close the tab or browser, and all session state is erased forever.

EasyList Ad Blocking

Pre-packaged with EasyList & EasyPrivacy rules that automatically intercept and block tracking beacons, banners, and video ads before they load.

GPC & Anti-Fingerprint

Enforces Global Privacy Control (GPC) and Do Not Track (DNT) headers on every request, keeping your digital footprint untraceable.
